The Indiana University Jetstream2 supercomputer center, where Dendra is hosted, will be offline for essential, extended maintenance to apply critical operating system and networking updates. This is a multi-day scheduled outage. Dendra servers will be shut down gracefully starting around 3:30 AM Pacific Time on Tuesday, August 4, and we expect to bring services back online around 2:30 PM Pacific Time on Wednesday, August 5. For more information visit https://support.access-ci.org/node/12247 and https://jetstream.status.io/. Updates will be posted on this status page.
